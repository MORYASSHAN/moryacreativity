01 — 仲間たち

Meet the herd (08)

Eight original friends, each 28 cm of recycled-bottle plush and hand-finished in Osaka. Every one has a temperament, a favourite nap spot and a digital twin that only wakes for its owner.

02 — 使い方

From cloud box to cosy island

Follow the paw prints. A FUWA starts as something you can squeeze and grows into something you can visit.

  1. Live 01

    Adopt a friend

    Choose from the herd. Your FUWA arrives napping in a recyclable cloud box with a stitched birth card and a name you can change exactly once.

  2. Live 02

    Tap the paw tag

    Hold your phone to the left back paw. Its digital twin blinks awake — a one-of-one collectible tied to the stitch number on the tag, owned by you, no wallet jargon required.

  3. Live 03

    Grow your herd

    Dress them in seasonal knits, log hugs, swap twins with friends. Twins remember every nap they've taken with you and level up their snore.

  4. Spring 2027 04

    Fuwa Island

    A slow, shared island where twins visit each other's homes. Owners vote on the next three animals — the axolotl lobby is already very loud.
